## Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a structured AI prompt system that can
generate SEO-optimized blog content and content clusters for a real
local business.

The system is designed to support keyword research, search intent
mapping, SEO blog planning, long-form content generation, internal
linking, and local SEO.

---

## Business Used

**The Beans & Beyond Cafe**

**Location:** Chandkheda, Ahmedabad, Gujarat

The business is a cafe offering coffee, beverages, food, desserts and
multiple cuisine options. Public business listings identify its
location near Zundal on Sardar Patel Ring Road and describe it as a
vegetarian cafe with indoor, outdoor and rooftop seating.

---

## Project Objective

The objective is to develop a reusable prompt workflow that can:

- Identify relevant SEO keywords
- Map keywords to search intent
- Generate SEO-friendly blog outlines
- Create long-form pillar content
- Generate supporting blog content
- Build a content cluster
- Suggest internal links
- Adapt content for local SEO
- Maintain a helpful and business-focused writing style

## Planned Deliverables

### 1. Business Research
Basic information about the selected business and its SEO requirements.

### 2. Keyword Research
Primary, secondary and long-tail keywords with search intent.

### 3. Content Cluster
One pillar topic supported by related blog topics.

### 4. Prompt System
Reusable prompts for keyword analysis, blog outlines, blog generation
and internal linking.

### 5. Pillar Blog
A comprehensive SEO-focused article targeting the primary topic.

### 6. Supporting Blogs
Three to five related articles supporting the pillar topic.

### 7. Internal Linking Strategy
Suggested connections between the pillar and supporting content.

### 8. Local SEO
Content optimized around relevant Ahmedabad and local service searches.

# Prompt 01 — Keyword & Search Intent Analysis

## Purpose

This prompt analyzes a list of SEO keywords and identifies their
search intent, keyword type, user need and suitable content format.

---

## Reusable Prompt

You are an SEO content strategist.

Analyze the following business information and keyword list.

### Business
{{BUSINESS_NAME}}

### Location
{{BUSINESS_LOCATION}}

### Business Category
{{BUSINESS_CATEGORY}}

### Keywords
{{KEYWORD_LIST}}

For each keyword, provide:

1. Keyword
2. Search intent:
   - Informational
   - Commercial
   - Transactional
   - Navigational
   - Local
3. User's likely need
4. Keyword type:
   - Primary
   - Secondary
   - Long-tail
   - Local
5. Recommended content type
6. Suggested target page or blog topic

Do not invent search-volume numbers.

Keep the analysis focused on realistic user searches related to the
business, its services/products and location.

Present the final result in a clear table.

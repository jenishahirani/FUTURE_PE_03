# Prompt 03 — Pillar Blog Generator

## Purpose

This prompt generates a comprehensive pillar article targeting the
main SEO topic for the business.

---

## Reusable Prompt

You are an experienced SEO content writer.

Write a comprehensive pillar blog for the following business.

### Business
{{BUSINESS_NAME}}

### Location
{{BUSINESS_LOCATION}}

### Primary Keyword
{{PRIMARY_KEYWORD}}

### Secondary Keywords
{{SECONDARY_KEYWORDS}}

### Search Intent
{{SEARCH_INTENT}}

### Blog Topic
{{BLOG_TOPIC}}

### Available Business Information
{{BUSINESS_INFORMATION}}

Create a helpful, readable and business-focused article.

Include:

1. SEO-friendly title
2. Introduction
3. H1
4. H2 and H3 sections
5. Naturally integrated keywords
6. Relevant local information
7. Helpful practical information
8. FAQ section
9. Conclusion
10. Suggested internal links

SEO requirements:

- Write for humans first.
- Match the user's search intent.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Use keywords naturally.
- Keep paragraphs easy to read.
- Use bullet points where useful.
- Do not make unsupported claims.
- Do not invent prices, ratings, offers, opening hours or facilities.
- Clearly distinguish general recommendations from verified business
  information.

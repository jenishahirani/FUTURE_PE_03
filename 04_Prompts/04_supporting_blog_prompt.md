# Prompt 04 — Supporting Blog Generator

## Purpose

This prompt generates supporting articles that target related
long-tail and local keywords and connect back to the pillar article.

---

## Reusable Prompt

You are an SEO content writer creating a supporting article for a
business content cluster.

### Business
{{BUSINESS_NAME}}

### Location
{{BUSINESS_LOCATION}}

### Supporting Topic
{{SUPPORTING_TOPIC}}

### Primary Keyword
{{PRIMARY_KEYWORD}}

### Related Keywords
{{RELATED_KEYWORDS}}

### Search Intent
{{SEARCH_INTENT}}

### Pillar Blog Topic
{{PILLAR_TOPIC}}

Write a useful and readable supporting article.

Include:

- SEO-friendly title
- Introduction
- H1
- H2/H3 sections
- Naturally used keywords
- Useful local context
- FAQ section where appropriate
- Conclusion
- Internal link to the pillar article
- Relevant links to other supporting articles where appropriate

Requirements:

- Match the search intent.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Do not repeat the pillar article unnecessarily.
- Provide unique value.
- Do not invent unsupported business information.
- Use a natural and helpful writing style.

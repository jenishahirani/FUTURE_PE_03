# Prompt 05 — Internal Linking Strategy

## Purpose

This prompt creates an internal linking plan connecting the pillar
article with supporting articles.

---

## Reusable Prompt

You are an SEO content strategist.

Create an internal linking strategy for the following content cluster.

### Business
{{BUSINESS_NAME}}

### Pillar Article
{{PILLAR_ARTICLE}}

### Supporting Articles
{{SUPPORTING_ARTICLES}}

For each article, provide:

1. Source article
2. Target article
3. Suggested anchor text
4. Reason for the link
5. Recommended placement

Requirements:

- Use descriptive and natural anchor text.
- Avoid excessive exact-match keyword anchors.
- Links should be useful to readers.
- Every supporting article should connect to the pillar where
  relevant.
- Identify useful links between supporting articles.
- Do not create links that are irrelevant to the content.

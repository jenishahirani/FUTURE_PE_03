## Business

## Location

---

## Keyword Research Objective

The objective of this keyword research is to identify relevant SEO
keywords that potential customers may use when searching for cafes,
coffee, food, desserts and related services in Chandkheda and Ahmedabad.

The keywords will later be used for search intent mapping, content
cluster planning and SEO blog generation.

## Keyword Intent Categories

### Commercial Intent

Users are looking for a cafe, food or dining option and may be
considering visiting a business.

Examples:
- cafe in Chandkheda
- cafes near Zundal
- rooftop cafe in Chandkheda
- coffee shop in Chandkheda

### Informational Intent

Users are looking for information or ideas related to cafes,
food, coffee or dining.

Examples:
- best coffee and dessert combinations
- places to eat in Chandkheda
- rooftop cafes in Ahmedabad

### Local Intent

Users specifically include a location in their search or are looking
for a nearby business.

Examples:
- cafe in Chandkheda Ahmedabad
- cafes near Zundal Ahmedabad
- cafe near Sardar Patel Ring Road

---

## Keyword Selection Strategy

Keywords were selected based on:

1. Business category
2. Products and services offered by the cafe
3. Location of the business
4. Local search behavior
5. Commercial and informational search intent
6. Potential for creating supporting SEO content

Search-volume figures have not been added at this stage because they
have not been independently verified using a dedicated keyword-volume
tool.

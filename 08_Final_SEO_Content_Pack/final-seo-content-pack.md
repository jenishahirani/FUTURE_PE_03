## Project

AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator for Business Websites

## Business

**The Beans & Beyond Cafe**

**Location:** Chandkheda, Ahmedabad, Gujarat

---

## 1. Primary SEO Topic

**Best Cafes in Ahmedabad**

This topic is used as the main focus of the content cluster and is supported by related cafe, food, coffee, ambience, and local-search topics.

---

## 2. Target Audience

The content is intended for:

* People searching for cafes in Ahmedabad
* Students looking for comfortable cafes
* Professionals looking for places to work or meet
* Friends and families looking for casual dining spaces
* Coffee and food lovers
* Local customers exploring cafes near Chandkheda and Ahmedabad

---

## 3. Search Intent

The primary intent is **commercial investigation and informational search**.

Users may be researching cafes before deciding where to visit. They may also be looking for information about cafe food, drinks, ambience, location, and overall experience.

## 7. Internal Linking

The pillar blog connects with the supporting blogs through relevant anchor text.

Supporting blogs can also link to other related supporting articles where appropriate.

This creates a connected content cluster that improves navigation and helps organize related information.

---

## 8. SEO Content Guidelines

The generated content should:

* Provide useful information to readers.
* Use keywords naturally.
* Avoid unnecessary keyword stuffing.
* Use clear headings and subheadings.
* Include relevant local keywords.
* Maintain a natural and helpful writing style.
* Use internal links where relevant.
* Match content with search intent.
* Focus on genuine user value.

---

## 9. Prompt Workflow

The complete workflow follows these stages:

**Business Research → Keyword Research → Search Intent → Content Cluster → Prompt Engineering → Pillar Blog → Supporting Blogs → Internal Linking → Local SEO → Final SEO Content Pack**

This workflow can be reused for other local businesses by changing the business information, target audience, keywords, location, and content topics.

---

## 10. Final Outcome

The project produces a structured SEO content system containing business research, keyword research, content planning, reusable prompts, pillar content, supporting blogs, internal linking, and local SEO recommendations.

The system is designed to make AI-assisted content creation more organized, repeatable, and focused on search intent and business needs.

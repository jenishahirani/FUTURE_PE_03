# Internal Linking Strategy

Internal linking connects related pages within the website and helps users discover useful content. It can also help search engines understand the relationship between different pages.

## Pillar Blog

## Supporting-to-Supporting Links

Supporting blogs can also link to one another where the topics are naturally related. For example, a blog discussing cafe food can link to another article about popular drinks, while an article about choosing a cafe can link to content about ambience and cafe experiences.

## Internal Linking Best Practices

* Use descriptive anchor text.
* Link only when the destination page is relevant.
* Avoid excessive internal links.
* Connect supporting articles back to the main pillar topic.
* Keep links useful for readers.
* Review links whenever new content is added.

## Conclusion

The internal linking structure creates a connected content cluster in which the pillar blog serves as the main topic and supporting blogs provide additional information. This structure improves content navigation and helps organize the website's SEO content around relevant cafe-related topics.
